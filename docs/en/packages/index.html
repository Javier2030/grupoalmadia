Cheap gets paid for twice.

  • A badly created profile gets suspended. Getting it back takes weeks and sometimes you have to start over, losing the reviews.
  • A spoofed email costs your customers money: the fraudster sends your invoice with a different bank account and the customer comes back to you.
  • Without a tested backup there is no backup: many companies find out on the day of the incident that it was never possible to restore.
  • We hand you a report of what was reviewed, what was fixed and what is still open, with data you can verify yourself.
  • What we do not do: fake reviews, promises of a top ranking, penetration tests, or legal or accounting opinions.

What we deliver can be checked.

We do not guarantee rankings on Google or that you will never be attacked. We guarantee evidence of every single thing we did.

  • Get Found: if within 30 days the profile is not verified and the website is not indexed by Google, 100% of the setup fee is refunded.
  • Protected Business: each block is delivered with its proof —DMARC report, list of accounts with two-factor authentication, restore record—. If a test fails, it is fixed at no cost.
  • Data in Order: if a gap appears in the documents within the following 90 days, they are adjusted at no cost.
  • No lock-in: monthly plans can be cancelled with 15 days' notice. The accounts and the domain stay in your company's name.

Do you work with companies in other countries?

Yes. All the work is done online: Google Business Profile, email, backups, website and documents. We work in whatever time zone suits you and deliver by email and video call.

Why does this cost more than someone charging half?

Because we deliver the verification resolved, the profile complete, the website that backs it up and a report with numbers. Whoever charges half creates the profile and leaves; you pay again three months later.

Do you guarantee the top spot on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. The order depends on reviews, proximity and relevance. We control relevance completely and leave you the system for getting reviews.

Do you do ethical hacking or penetration testing?

No. We do digital hygiene: email, accounts, backups, website and training, which is how the vast majority of attacks on a small business get in.

What do you mean, someone can impersonate my email?

If your domain has no SPF, DKIM and DMARC, anyone can send emails that look like yours and ask your customers for money. It is fixed in hours and it is the first thing we check.

How is payment handled?

Half to start and half on delivery, by international transfer. Monthly plans are billed in arrears and can be cancelled whenever you want.

We tell you what is missing before we charge you.
